Preparing offline player…

Saved songs work offline in this browser. Keep Gnune open during transfer and connect Tailscale to return listening progress. Your phone may remove browser storage to free space. Original audio format support depends on your browser.

optional: Home Screen app

Pair in Safari first, then use Share → Add to Home Screen. New Home Screen apps on iOS 17.2 or later can recover the same pairing online. Existing icons without a saved pairing must pair once there. Android offers Install app. Saved songs stay in the browser or app where they played.

If Safari shows a plain G icon, open while connected, close all Gnune tabs and the Home Screen app, then reopen Safari and check Share → Add to Home Screen. Keep the existing app and its saved songs until you have verified the new icon and pairing; do not clear website data.
